🚗 NAVISENSE-IDR — How It Works

10 steps from GPS to AI-powered dead reckoning:

1
📍 Origin & Destination Selection
User selects start location and destination. GPS fix is acquired as the authoritative position source.
2
🚗 Journey Begins
Navigation starts. The vehicle puck follows the calculated route in real-time.
3
🟢 GNSS Signal Active
NavIC/GPS satellites provide accurate positioning. Vehicle puck displays exact real-world coordinates.
4
🔴 Tunnel / GNSS Signal Loss
Vehicle enters a tunnel or GNSS-denied zone. Satellite signal drops to zero. The vehicle does NOT stop.
5
🧠 AI + IMU Sensors Engage
Accelerometer, gyroscope, and magnetometer data is processed by the GRU neural network for velocity estimation.
6
📍 Dead Reckoning Active
Even without GPS, the system continuously estimates position using IMU + AI. The vehicle puck keeps moving accurately.
7
🗺️ Map Matching + EKF Fusion
Extended Kalman Filter fuses AI predictions with road geometry constraints to minimize drift.
8
🚗 Continuous Movement
Navigation continues seamlessly through the tunnel. Position accuracy degrades gracefully over time.
9
🟢 GNSS Signal Restored
Vehicle exits the tunnel. Satellite signals are reacquired. System begins smooth re-synchronization.
10
🔄 Smooth Recovery
Position blends gradually from DR estimate to authoritative GPS — no jarring jumps on the map.
